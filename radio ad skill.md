# 30-Second Radio Ad Script

Drafts a broadcast-ready 30-second radio script. Use when a producer has a brief (candidate, race, message, target audience) and needs a first cut.

## Rules

- **Hard limit: 75 words of voiceover, max.** Most natural-paced VO clocks around 150 words per minute, so 75 words ≈ 30 seconds with breathing room. Going over runs long, period.
- **Open with a hook in the first 5 seconds.** A name, a question, a vivid image — not a generic "Are you tired of..." This is radio; listeners can leave at any second.
- **The candidate's name appears at least twice.** Once early, once in the close. Name recognition is half the job of a radio ad.
- **Required disclaimer at the end.** "Paid for by [committee], not authorized by any candidate or candidate's committee" — or the candidate-authorized version if applicable. Get this from the producer's brief; don't guess.
- **One argument per ad.** If the brief lists three points, pick the strongest and propose the other two as separate spots. A 30-second ad that tries to do three things does none of them.
- **Tone matches the race.** A challenger spot sounds urgent; an incumbent spot sounds confident. Match what the producer's brief specifies — if it doesn't specify, ask.
- **Don't:** rhyme, use cute wordplay, or write punchlines. Radio attack ads especially: dry, specific, sourced.

## Format

```
TITLE: [Working title]
CANDIDATE: [Name]
LENGTH: :30
TARGET: [Audience]

[0:00-0:05] MUSIC: [cue]
VO: [opening line]

[0:05-0:25] VO: [body, ~55 words]
MUSIC: [under, swelling at...]

[0:25-0:30] VO: [close + tag]
DISCLAIMER: [paid for by line]
```

Word count goes at the bottom. If over 75, flag and ask the producer what to cut.
